01 / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is this project?

The Reverse Engineering - Brain It On! Project is about hidden functions, features, and properties like modifiers, object scaling, indexes, and more. The project was started before we cracked the code, and we conducted in-depth research to understand and perfect it.

Why was this project created?

Regarding the Basemented story, we're kind of suspicious about how it was created and how it works. That's why we're digging deeper into it, testing different ideas, and experimenting until we can get it working as perfectly as possible.

Did the developer/owner of Brain It On! approve these features?

Yes. The developer/owner has accepted these features, but they should be used with limitations.

What are the limitations of these experiments?

These experiments can have a significant impact on the gameplay experience. Once a trick becomes known, many people may ask for it, try to use it, or demand access to it. If these features are used too widely, they could potentially disrupt the game or negatively affect the experience for everyone. For that reason, some experiments should be used carefully and kept limited.

02 / HOW TO USE

How to Use

Live Art Studio

Step 1: Open the Live Art Studio project.

Step 2: Choose your pixel that you like and create your drawing inside the pixel area.

Step 3: Export or apply the result to your level.

Matrix Modifier

Step 1: Open the Matrix Modifier project.

Step 2: Enter the values you want to modify.

Step 3: Apply the generated matrix data.

Rich Text Title Injector

Step 1: Choose a title type.

Step 2: Enter your text and formatting.

Step 3: Generate the title data.

Outside the Walls

Step 1: Select the object and map location.

Step 2: Drag the object in 1/4-grid increments.

Step 3: Generate the code and copy the layout.
